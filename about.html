Howard Donnell

Founder, writer, producer and director

Howard founded Narragate on a simple belief: a good story closes the distance between people.

He writes songs as well as scripts. His catalog supplies the soundtracks for Duet and Aileen, so the music in a Narragate film comes from the same hand as the screenplay.

He has worked on film sets in Hollywood, taught in China, staged work in small-town theaters and collaborated across borders. More than twenty years of filmmaking and teaching have left him drawn to one kind of character: people with real faults who still earn an audience’s care.

Story first

Everything begins with the script and answers to it. If the page doesn’t hold, nothing built on it will.

Redemptive truth

We go looking for stories that face what is broken and still leave room for grace and a second chance.

Emotional integrity

People contradict themselves, hide things and go quiet. We write them that way.

Artistic excellence

Independent describes the budget, not the standard. The bar stays high at every stage.

Lean, purposeful production

Money goes on the screen. Crews stay small, decisions are shared, and nothing is there for show.

Philosophical vision

Our worldview is shaped by faith. It keeps us looking for truth, beauty and meaning, often where few expect them.

Community and collaboration

A film takes many hands. We look for artists, producers and investors who care about meaning and about the market.
