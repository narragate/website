Privacy

What the forms on this site collect, and what happens to it.

Last updated 21 October 2026

Narragate Entertainment LLC runs this site from West Lawn, Pennsylvania. There are two forms on it: the contact form and the investor packet request. They are the only places the site asks you for anything.

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does not track you between visits or across other sites. Nothing you do here is sold or shared for advertising.

What the forms collect

Only what you type:

Contact form
Your name, your email address and your message.
Investor form
Your name, your email address, and optionally your organization, your role and a message. Ticking the consent box is recorded with the request.

Organization, role and message are optional on the investor form. Name and email are not, because without them there is no way to reply.

What is recorded with a submission

When a form is sent, three further things are stored alongside it: the IP address it came from, the country that address resolves to, and the browser's user-agent string. These exist to stop automated abuse — they are what lets us cap how many submissions one address can make in an hour. They are not used to build a profile of you.

The time of the submission is recorded too.

Where it goes

A submission is stored first and sent second, so that a message cannot be lost if the email fails. Three companies are involved, and none of them receive anything beyond what is described above:

Cloudflare
Stores submissions, and runs the anti-spam check that appears above the send button. Its privacy notice is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
Resend
Delivers the notification email to Howard. Its privacy notice is at resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.
GitHub
Hosts the pages themselves and keeps standard server logs of requests. Its privacy notice is at docs.github.com.

The email then sits in Howard's mailbox, which is hosted by Apple.

How long it is kept

Form submissions are kept for two years and then deleted. The counters used for rate limiting expire after two hours. Correspondence that follows from an enquiry is kept for as long as there is a reason to keep it, and investor records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

What your browser stores

Two small values are saved in your browser's session storage: which song the player has open, and whether you have already seen the opening animation. They are cleared when you close the tab, they never leave your device, and we cannot read them. No cookies are set.

Asking to see or delete what we hold

Write to howard.donnell@narragate.com and ask. You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, and we will do it. No particular form of words is needed.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the lawful basis for handling a form submission is your consent, given when you send it, and you may withdraw that consent at any time by writing to the address above.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Links out

Some pages link to films on YouTube. Nothing from YouTube is embedded here, so YouTube learns nothing about you until you choose to follow one of those links. Once you do, you are on their site and their terms apply.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
