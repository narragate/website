The story

Ethan is a songwriter with a reputation and not much else: a barroom regular whose best work seems to be behind him. Annie sings in a church choir and keeps her guard up.

When he hears her at rehearsal, he knows he has found the other half of a song he can’t finish alone. They write. A recording goes to Nashville. Their first duet, at a local café, should be the start of something, and it collides with a car accident that nearly kills him.

What follows is the harder story: his retreat, her refusal to wait quietly, and the question of whether two people who found a common voice can keep it.
